PULSE SYNC 1.0.7
Original OP-1 with Opus 2 on firmware #246

Replace pulsesync.opmod in mods, eject and restart.
Select line in, keep input active and choose Sync on the tempo screen.
Feed a line-level pulse signal. Attenuate modular CV before connecting it.

SETTINGS
Opus menu > T2 > Pulse Sync > blue push

Pulse Threshold 1–10 (default 5)
1 detects weaker signals. 10 requires stronger signals.
Raise it for false ticks; lower it for missed pulses.
The old raw threshold setting resets to 5 when upgrading.

AutoStart off/on (default off)
On sends MIDI Start with the first selected pulse.
When pulse clock stops, it sends MIDI Stop after two expected pulse
intervals, with a minimum wait of 100 ms and a maximum of 4 seconds.
After a single pulse, the acquisition timeout is 4 seconds.
Off gives manual play/stop.

Clock Priority USB/Pulse (default USB)
Chooses the preferred source while both clocks are active.
Falls back to the other source when the preferred clock disappears.
USB clock is considered absent after 500 ms.
USB timing/transport messages are ignored while pulse clock is selected.
USB takes responsibility for transport when it becomes the selected source.
AutoStart/AutoStop applies while pulse clock controls playback.

Input Rate
24ppq (default), 48ppq, 96ppq, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32.
All settings feed 24 PPQ MIDI clock to the OP-1.
Note divisions need two pulses to measure the interval.

Mute Clock Input defaults to on and mutes only Line In in Sync mode.
On Shift + Mic, a small blue Pulse Sync label shows that mute is enabled
for the selected Line In source. Pulse Polarity selects positive/negative.
The panel displays USB, pulse or waiting for the selected clock source.

The owner confirmed on an original OP-1 that 1.0.1 restores pulse tempo lock after USB MIDI use. The firmware signature checks and host tests passed. Automatic transport, simultaneous clock sources and CPU load still await hardware testing.
