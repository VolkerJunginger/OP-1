PULSE SYNC 0.1.3 — QUICK MANUAL
Original OP-1 with Opus 2
ID: esc.pulsesync | Author: ESC_CODE

1. Replace the old pulsesync.opmod in mods, eject and restart.
   Remove any other old Pulse Sync copy. The new ID starts fresh settings.
2. Feed a line-level 24 PPQ clock into the audio input.
   Attenuate modular CV first; do not connect raw CV directly.
3. Select line in with SHIFT + MIC and keep the input active.
4. Select Sync on the tempo screen. Play/stop stays manual.

SETTINGS
Opus menu > T2 > Pulse Sync > blue push:
- Pulse sync: on/off.
- Mute clock input: on hides the clicks while sync continues.
- Pulse threshold: lower for missed pulses; raise for false triggers.
- Pulse polarity: positive or negative edge.

Defaults: sync on, mute on, threshold 1024, positive polarity.
