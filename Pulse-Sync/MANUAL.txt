Pulse Sync 1.1.9
Original OP-1 firmware #246 with Opus core 2.58 or newer.

Copy pulsesync.opmod to mods, eject and restart. Select Line In and set
the OP-1 tempo to Sync. Feed line-level clock pulses. Attenuate modular CV.

Open Pulse Sync settings with a blue push on its row in the Opus mods list.
Select Sync Input: L+R (default), L or R. Pulse Sync listens to and mutes
the selected channel(s). In L or R, the other channel remains available
for audio. Turn Pulse Sync Off to pass both channels. Shift + Mic shows
the selected sync input in blue.

Set Input Rate to 24, 48 or 96 PPQ, or 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32.
Pulse Threshold is 1–10. Higher settings require stronger pulses.
Pulse Polarity selects positive or negative edges.
AutoStart optionally starts and stops playback with the pulse clock.
Clock Priority selects the preferred source when USB and Pulse are active.
